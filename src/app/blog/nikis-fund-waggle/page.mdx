## It started with Niki

If you follow <TextLink href="https://instagram.com/nikifromthebay">Niki</TextLink>, you might know that my cat
needed an expensive knee surgery.

We were lucky enough to be able to pay for it. But it made me think about all the families who can't.
Nobody should have to choose between paying their rent and saving their pet.

That's why we created <TextLink href="https://www.waggle.org/sponsors/niki-s-fund">Niki's Fund</TextLink>.

## What is Waggle?

<TextLink href="https://www.waggle.org">Waggle</TextLink> is a 501c3 non profit crowdfunding platform that helps
pet owners who are struggling financially pay for emergency veterinary care.

When a pet needs treatment that the family can't afford, a campaign is created on Waggle.
Donors can then give directly to help that specific animal.

## What does Niki's Fund do?

Niki's Fund supports pet owners who face unexpected illnesses or accidents, with treatment costs beyond what most
families can afford.

> Our goal is simple: making sure money never prevents beloved animal companions from getting the care they need.

## The pets we helped

You can see all the pets Niki's Fund has helped on <TextLink href="https://www.waggle.org/sponsors/niki-s-fund">our Waggle sponsor page</TextLink>.

Every one of them is a pet that got a second chance, and a family that didn't have to say goodbye too early.
